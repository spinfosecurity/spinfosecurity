How to use this

Built for plant engineers, OT defenders, and integrators who need a fast answer to “is this my gear?”

  1. Search your vendor or product family first.
  2. Prioritize Critical and High before Medium/Low.
  3. Open the CISA page for affected versions and vendor fixes.
  4. Subscribe to the RSS feed if you want changes in your reader or ticket queue.

This page aggregates public CISA CSAF documents. It is not a substitute for vendor bulletins or your change-control process. Always verify affected products and versions on the official advisory before patching or isolating systems.
